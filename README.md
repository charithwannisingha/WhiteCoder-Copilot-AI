**WhiteCoder Copilot** is an advanced AI-powered interview assistant built for developers and professionals. It provides real-time speech transcription, instant translation, and AI-crafted answers streamed word-by-word to help you ace your technical interviews.

🌐 **Visit Website:** [https://copilot.whitecoder.online/](https://copilot.whitecoder.online/)

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🎙️ **Real-time Transcription:** Powered by Deepgram Nova-3, it captures every word from the interviewer with ultra-low latency (<300ms).
* 🤖 **AI Answer Generation:** Utilizes Gemini 2.5 Flash to craft confident and concise English answers in real-time.
* 🌍 **Multi-Language Translation:** Instantly translates the interviewer's questions into 6 native languages (Sinhala, Tamil, Chinese, French, Spanish, German).
* 🎧 **Tab Audio Capture:** Seamlessly works with Zoom, Google Meet, and MS Teams by simply sharing your browser tab audio—no extra plugins required.
* 🤫 **Smart Silence Detection:** Advanced Voice Activity Detection (VAD) triggers the AI exactly when the interviewer finishes asking a question.
* 🔒 **Private by Design:** All audio processing is per-session and instantly discarded. No third-party data storage.

---

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. **Choose Language & Launch:** Select your preferred native language and click "Launch Copilot".
2. **Share Tab Audio:** Click "Start" and share your active meeting tab (e.g., Zoom/Meet) while ensuring "Share Tab Audio" is ticked.
3. **Read Your Answers:** As the interviewer speaks, you will see the live transcript. Once they finish, the AI instantly streams the suggested answer for you to read and speak.

---

## 💻 Technologies Used

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
* **Audio Processing:** Browser Web Audio API
* **Speech-to-Text Integration:** Deepgram API
* **AI Generation & Translation:** Gemini API
